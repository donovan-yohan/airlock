End-to-end timing

Where the human enters the loop

The reviewer inspects request/plan digests, every proposed argv element, trusted-local policy labels, and risk warnings, then approves that exact plan or denies it. Airlock stages matching opened gh and authentication inputs, revalidates its canonical Bubblewrap launcher, then persists approval, immutable plan, and running attempt before execution.

Receipt semantics

What each state actually proves

StateWhat it provesWhat it does not prove
pendingThe requester persisted an exact profile/argv proposal.Review, approval, or execution.
approved_for_executionA trusted reviewer authorized one persisted plan digest.That the provider action ran or succeeded.
deniedThe trusted side or reviewer declined the request.Any external change.
executedThe trusted child returned success and terminal state persisted.It does not prove provider state: whether the provider accepted it or the intended effect exists.

Failure paths

Failures stop before authority drifts

  • Expired, replayed, malformed, hostile-text, stale-config, or profile-invalid proposals fail closed.
  • A reviewer can reject without exposing credentials, resolved paths, or child output to the requester.
  • Concurrent/replayed POSTs cannot create a second running provider invocation.
  • If execution fails or becomes uncertain, no executed receipt is emitted; the reviewer may explicitly retry and verify.
  • Bounded stdout/stderr previews are sanitized and credential-redacted for trusted review only; receipts and requester/MCP/Hermes never include them.
  • Bubblewrap confines filesystem/process access to pinned invocation inputs, but network and host loopback remain shared: this is not an egress firewall. Broad approval is reviewer-approved RCE.
  • External verification remains independent of the human-entered outcome receipt.
